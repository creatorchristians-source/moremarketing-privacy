Privacy Policy

More Marketing LLC · Effective October 21, 2026

01Who we are

More Marketing LLC ("MoreMarketing", "we", "us") is a marketing agency based in Colorado, USA. We run advertising, funnels and sales systems for the coaching businesses we partner with. This policy explains what information we handle, including through the MoreMarketing app that connects to Meta's Marketing API, and how we protect it.

02What our Meta app does

Our app is an internal tool. It is used only by MoreMarketing staff, and only on ad accounts, pages and Instagram accounts whose owners have granted MoreMarketing access through Meta Business Manager. It lets us:

  • Create and manage campaigns, ad sets, ads and ad creatives.
  • Upload the images and videos our clients supply for their ads.
  • Build and manage custom and lookalike audiences on a client's behalf.
  • Read campaign performance so we can report results to the client.

The app is not offered to the public, and it does not let anyone log in with Facebook.

03Information we handle

  • Ad account data: campaign settings, budgets, targeting, creatives and performance metrics for accounts we manage.
  • Page and Instagram identifiers: the IDs needed to publish ads under a client's page.
  • Customer lists: when a client asks us to build an audience from their own customer or lead list, the emails and phone numbers are hashed (SHA-256) on our machine before upload. Meta receives only the hashed values.
  • Business contact details: names, emails and phone numbers of the clients and team members we work with.

We do not collect information from people who simply see or click an ad. Ad delivery and measurement are handled by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy.

04How we use it

We use this information only to run, measure and improve advertising for the client who owns it. We never sell personal information, never share one client's data with another, and never use it to build profiles for anyone else.

05Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms, to deliver and measure the ads.
  • Service providers we rely on to operate, such as cloud hosting and CRM tools, bound by their own confidentiality and security terms.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

06How long we keep it

We keep ad account data for as long as we manage that account, and for reporting and record keeping afterwards as the law requires. Uploaded customer lists are deleted from our systems once the audience is built, unless the client asks us to keep a copy.

07Security

Access tokens and credentials are stored outside public code, separately for each client. Access to client accounts is limited to the people doing the work, and a client can revoke our access at any time from their Meta Business Manager.

08Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete information we hold about you. To have your data removed, contact the MoreMarketing team member you work with, or message More Marketing LLC through any of our official channels, and include the ad account or business name involved. We will confirm deletion within 30 days. You can also remove our app's access at any time in Facebook under Settings and privacy, then Business Integrations.

09Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
